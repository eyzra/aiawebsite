About the school

A school your neighbours already know

Abuja International Academy opened in January 1997, founded by Evangelist G.F.I. Amu and shaped in its early years by the educationist Mr. J.A. Imoudu, known to generations of pupils as Baba. Nasarawa State granted formal approval on 5 March 1999. NECO recognised the school as an examination centre in 2000, WAEC in 2004.

Nearly thirty years in one community leaves a record. Ask around Mararaba and you will find a parent whose child passed through our gates, and some of those children now bring us their own. What keeps families coming back is simple enough to say in a sentence: teaching that is taken seriously, conduct that is taken just as seriously, and a fee schedule that does not put either out of reach.

  • Academic excellence

    Results-driven learning, measured against the highest standard rather than the nearest one.

  • Top-notch teaching

    Skilled, prepared educators in every classroom, in every subject, on every campus.

  • Moral discipline

    Character and conduct that set the example, from the youngest pupil to the senior staff.

  • Staff welfare

    People who are cared for, so that they can care for your child properly.

  • Honesty and integrity

    Doing right, especially when nobody is watching.

Admissions

How admission works

We admit into every level at all three campuses, through the year, wherever places remain.

  1. Tell us about your child

    Fill the form below, send a WhatsApp message, or call the office. We need the child's age and the class you have in mind.

  2. Visit the campus

    Come on a working day. You will meet the head of the campus and see the classrooms your child would sit in.

  3. Entrance assessment

    Pupils applying from Primary 4 upward sit a short test in English and Mathematics so we can place them correctly.

  4. Offer and fees

    We confirm the place in writing, hand you the fee schedule and the uniform list, and agree a resumption date.

What to bring

  • Birth certificate or age declaration
  • Four recent passport photographs
  • The last report card, and a transfer certificate where one applies
  • Immunisation record for creche and early years

Affordability is a promise we make in our slogan, so we would rather discuss fees plainly than hide them. Call the office and we will send you the current schedule for the class and campus you are asking about.
